The Lenses & The Data

Every record here comes from two places: real NFL data and real astronomy. The football numbers — scores, betting spreads, stadium roof, wind — come from nflverse, an open-source dataset covering every NFL game from 1999 to today (regular season and playoffs). Each game is then stamped with the state of the sky at its actual kickoff, computed from the game’s Eastern start time. We tally wins, losses, and spread covers by cosmic bucket. That’s the whole machine.

🌕

Moon Phase

Which of the eight traditional lunar phases the moon was in at kickoff, from New Moon to Waning Crescent.

Source: astronomical calculation (PyEphem), at game time.

♏

Zodiac Season

The tropical (Western) sun sign for the game’s date — “Scorpio season,” “Capricorn season,” and so on. Because the NFL runs September to February, only about six signs ever see football — there is no such thing as a game played in Leo season.

Source: the calendar date.

♈

Moon Sign

The zodiac sign the moon itself was passing through at kickoff. The moon crosses all twelve signs every month, so every sign gets a full share of games — the one lens where nothing is left empty.

Source: astronomical calculation (PyEphem), at game time.

♂

Mars Retrograde

Whether Mars, the planet of war, appeared to move backward at kickoff. Rarer than Mercury’s — about every two years, for roughly two months — so about one game in ten falls under it.

Source: astronomical calculation (PyEphem), at game time.

☿

Mercury Retrograde

Whether Mercury appeared to move backward across the sky at kickoff — the genuine astronomical event pop astrology loves to blame. It happens three to four times a year for about three weeks each, so roughly one game in five falls under it.

Source: astronomical calculation (PyEphem), at game time.

⬤

Moon Distance

How near or far the moon was from Earth at kickoff. Its orbit isn’t a circle, so distance swings between about 356,000 km (a Supermoon, near) and 407,000 km (a Micromoon, far). Each game is bucketed Supermoon, Average, or Micromoon.

Source: astronomical calculation (PyEphem), at game time.

The Wins / Against the Spread toggle counts outright results or cover results (spreads from nflverse, home-favorite convention). The year window and opponent filters narrow the same records. Every value is computed once per game and fixed — it never changes based on when the page is built or viewed.
